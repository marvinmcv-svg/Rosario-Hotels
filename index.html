Rooms with a story

Our rooms have names, not just numbers. Each one is dressed around a craft, a custom or a character of the Altiplano.

  • Cholitas For the Aymara women of the highlands, with their bowler hats and layered polleras. Titicaca
  • Chuspas The small woven pouch that carries coca leaves, hung in rows above the bed. Titicaca
  • Lluchus The knitted, ear-flapped hat of the Andes, in every colour of the market. Titicaca
  • Soles Carved sun discs for Inti, who rises every morning over the lake. Titicaca
  • Muñecos Hand-sewn Andean dolls keep watch from their niches. Titicaca
  • Cabaña A two-bedroom duplex cottage in the garden, under timber beams. Titicaca
  • Aguayo Altiplano textiles woven into lamps and art, with heated floors underfoot. Uyuni
